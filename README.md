# 🛒 E-Commerce Operations & Performance Marketing Analytics

## 📌 Overview
This project showcases end-to-end e-commerce operations, inventory planning, performance marketing optimization, and operational decision-making through a comprehensive business case study.

The analysis focuses on improving inventory health, maximizing advertising efficiency, and solving real-world operational challenges using data-driven strategies.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

### 📦 Part 1: Inventory Health & Replenishment Planning
- 🧹 Clean and standardize inventory data
- 🔍 Identify duplicates, missing values, and stock anomalies
- 📈 Calculate Rate of Sale (RoS)
- 🚀 Forecast peak-season demand
- 💰 Create replenishment recommendations within budget constraints

### 📢 Part 2: Ad Campaign Optimization
- 📊 Calculate CTR, CPC, CVR, ROAS, and ACOS
- ⚠️ Identify underperforming campaigns
- 💵 Reallocate marketing budgets efficiently
- 🎯 Recommend campaign scaling, pausing, and optimization actions

### 🛠️ Part 3: Operational Judgement & Business Scenarios
- 🚨 Amazon listing error resolution
- 📦 Warehouse fulfillment disruption management
- ⭐ Customer review sentiment analysis
- 🤝 Stakeholder communication and escalation planning
